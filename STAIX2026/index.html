STAI-X
Statistics and Trustworthy AI for
cross (X)-domain Acceleration

To integrate statistical science and artificial intelligence to accelerate trustworthy discovery and societal impact across diverse scientific domains.

Topics

We are soliciting paper submissions on all topics related to the interface of artificial intelligence and statistics, with a particular focus on problems motivated by applications. Possible topics include but are not limited to:

Foundation models, generative models and learning methods

Statistical and theoretical foundations of AI

Synthetic data

Scalability for resource-constrained training environments

Ethical AI

Benchmarking of AI-assisted tools and models

AI Agents and co-scientists for X

Study design for ML and AI

Integrating statistics and AI for: health and biological science, physical science and engineering, social science, business, and others
