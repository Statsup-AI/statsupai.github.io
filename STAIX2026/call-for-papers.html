Call for Papers & Posters

  • Papers: Submit a paper of up to 8 pages. All papers must be original and not simultaneously submitted to another journal or conference. Accepted papers will be considered for potential platform (oral) presentations, poster presentation and paper awards. In addition, a subset of top-ranked accepted papers will be eligible for fast-track review opportunities by partner journals through journal invitation. At certain point, authors of accepted papers will be asked to indicate their preferred partner journal for consideration of reviewing an expanded version of their work, although the authors are not required to submit a revision. Editors of each partner journal will then select suitable top-ranked papers based on quality and authors' preference for invitation to expedited review. Preprint server submissions are allowed without restrictions.
  • Posters(long abstracts): Submit a 2-page abstract. Parallel submissions to other journals/conferences are permitted. Accepted submissions will be eligible for poster presentations, lightning talks, and stellar abstract awards.
