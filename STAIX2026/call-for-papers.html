Topics

We are soliciting paper submissions on all topics related to the interface of artificial intelligence and statistics, with a particular focus on problems motivated by applications. Possible topics include but are not limited to:

Foundation models, generative models and statistical perspectives (Language models, diffusion models, etc.)

Statistical and theoretical foundations of AI

Trustworthy AI-driven scientific discovery (Diagnostics, Uncertainty quantification, etc.)

Synthetic data

Scalability for resource-constrained training environments

Ethical AI (alignment, privacy, fairness, etc)

Benchmarking of AI-assisted tools and models

AI Agents for enhancing researcher productivity and data analysis

AI-enhanced statistical data analysis tools

Study design for ML and AI (e.g., Bayesian optimization)

AI co-scientists and co-work in data science

Interventions and experiment design for machine learning and AI

Applications of AI data-driven methodology to scientific and business areas, including the biomedical sciences, social sciences, physical sciences, etc.

Integrating statistics and AI for computational biology

Education:
(1) New skills and curriculum for AI-enabled/assisted work - Efficiently integrating/controlling AI tools into workflow: coding/data analysis agents
(2) AI-enhanced education
