Call for Papers and Posters

Posters (long abstracts) can be simultaneously submitted to another journal or conference. Accepted posters will be considered for poster presentation, lightning talks, and stellar abstract awards.

All papers must be original and not simultaneously submitted to another journal or conference. Accepted papers will be considered for potential platform (oral) presentations, poster presentation and paper awards. In addition, a subset of top-ranked accepted papers will be eligible for fast-track review opportunities by partner journals through journal invitation. Shortly after the conference, authors of accepted papers will be asked to indicate their preferred partner journal for consideration of reviewing an expanded version of their work, although the authors are not required to submit a revision. Editors of each partner journal will then select suitable top-ranked papers based on quality and authors' preference for invitation to expedited review.

Preprint server submissions are allowed without restrictions. All accepted papers not published elsewhere will be offered the opportunity of publication in the STAI-X Proceedings.

Instructions for Submission

  • Papers: Submit a paper of up to 8 pages. Accepted papers will be eligible for being selected for platform (oral) presentations and paper awards. Moreover, a subset of accepted papers may be invited for fast-track review opportunities at selected ASA and IMS journals.
  • Posters: Submit a 2-page abstract. Parallel submissions to other conferences are permitted. Accepted submissions will be eligible for poster presentations, lightning talks, and stellar abstract awards.
