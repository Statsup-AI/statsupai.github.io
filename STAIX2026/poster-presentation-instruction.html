Instruction to Poster Presentations

Saturday, August 1, 2026  •  Harvard Science Center

Thank you for participating as a poster presenter at the upcoming STAI-X Conference! We are excited to showcase your work and look forward to this inaugural conference. Below are the key logistical details you will need for the day of the conference.

Note: The conference registration site has closed. Please check the Registration page for late or on-site registration.

📊 Your Assigned Poster Number Download the assignment sheet (Excel) 🗺️ Floor Plan Open the Science Center floor plan (PDF)

Date & Venue

  • Date: Saturday, August 1
  • Location: Harvard Science Center, 1 Oxford St., Cambridge, MA 02138
  • Your Assigned Poster Number: Link to the Excel Sheet
  • Floor Plan: Link — This floor plan maps out the poster number locations so you can easily find your designated spot when you arrive.

Poster Board Dimensions & Mounting

  • The display area in the posterboard stands measures 91" H x 44.75" W (approximately 7.58 ft H x 3.73 ft W).
  • Please note that the standard poster size of 3' high and 4' wide will work whether displayed in portrait or landscape orientation.
  • Magnets will be supplied onsite to attach your poster to the board.

Setup & Teardown Timing

  • Setup: The poster area will be open for setup starting at 12:30 PM and all posters must be fully set up before the official session begins at 5:35 PM. To ensure a smooth process, we highly recommend arriving to set up your poster during one of the following designated breaks:
    • 12:30 PM (Lunch Break)
    • 3:30 PM (Afternoon Coffee Break)
  • Teardown: Please ensure your poster is taken down at 7:30 PM.
