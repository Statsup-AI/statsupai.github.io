Registration

Registration for STAI-X 2026 is open through the ASA membersuite portal.

Register Now

Instructions

  • An account is free and required. ASA membership is NOT required.
  • Breakfast, coffee breaks, and lunch on August 1st are INCLUDED in the registration fee.
  • We value your inputs on our conference. Please fill out the optional survey questions.
  • Short courses are add-ons for additional fees. See the Short Courses tab for descriptions.
  • A lunch ticket option can be added for July 31. Be sure to click "Add" to purchase this option.
  • You may edit registration details such as answers to registration questions or purchase add-ons later.
