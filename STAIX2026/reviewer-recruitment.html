STAI-X is recruiting ACs and reviewers!

We are building a high-quality, community-driven review process and invite you to join us.

Area Chair (AC)

Eligibility

  • Senior researcher with an experience level commensurate with an Associate Professor or higher.

Commitment

Up to 10 papers or 20 posters per AC.

If you are interested, please contact us at staix.contact@gmail.com.

Reviewer

Eligibility

  1. Faculty, government, and industry researchers in Statistics, Biostatistics, Computer Science, and related fields.
  2. Senior doctoral students and postdoctoral researchers are welcome to participate in the peer-review process, by serving as trainee co-reviewers with their faculty supervisor reviewer. Faculty supervisor reviewers are required to explicitly acknowledge the participation of these trainee co-reviewers in their confidential comments to the area chairs.

Commitment

Up to 3 papers or 6 posters per reviewer.

If you are interested, please fill out this form.
