Candidate Statements for the “Stats Up Artificial Intelligence” Interest Group

About This Election

The Stats Up AI Interest Group is holding its election for Chair-Elect and Program Chair-Elect for 2027. Each office has one candidate. Candidate profiles and vision statements appear below, followed by officer appointments and continuing leadership. Only Chair-Elect and Program Chair-Elect are on the ballot.

Voting opens Monday, November 23, 2026 and closes Monday, December 7, 2026. Please review the candidate statements before voting. You may select “Abstain” for either office.

Candidate for Chair-Elect (2027)

Statement and profile information

Back to directory

Xihong Lin

Professor of Statistics and Chair of the Department of Statistics
Professor of Biostatistics, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health
Harvard University

Professional Profile
Vision Statement

Building on the strong foundation and accomplishments of previous leaders and members of the ASA AI Interest Group, my vision as Chair-Elect is to further advance the interface of statistics and AI and strengthen the role of statistics in the rapidly evolving AI landscape. I see this as a joint community effort that calls for collaboration among statisticians, AI researchers, and domain scientists. Building on the momentum of the inaugural STAI-X conference, I hope to promote foundational and methodological advances at the intersection of statistics and AI, as well as their applications to accelerate scientific discovery across disciplines. A central goal is to advance principled AI with rigorous statistical guarantees for reliability, uncertainty quantification, and trustworthy discovery. I also hope to foster scientific exchange and collaboration across academia, industry, and the broader scientific community.

Education is another important priority. AI presents both significant challenges and transformative opportunities for statistics education. We need to rethink the competencies students will need to thrive and become leaders in the age of AI, including strong foundations in statistics and AI, the ability to leverage AI effectively and responsibly, and the critical thinking and scientific judgment to evaluate AI-generated results. We should also engage in discussions on the implications of AI for curriculum and education policies and practices.

Ultimately, I hope to work together with the ASA AI Interest Group community to build on its existing strengths, expand its reach, and enhance the visibility and impact of statistics in shaping the future of AI and scientific discovery.

Candidate for Program Chair-Elect (2027)

Statement and profile information

Back to directory

Mladen Kolar

Professor of Data Sciences and Operations, USC Marshall School of Business
Visiting Professor of Statistics and Data Science, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence

Professional Profile
Vision Statement

Stats Up AI brings statisticians together to advance AI research and its applications through conferences, webinars, and shared resources. As Program Chair-Elect, I would support the continued success of STAI-X and the webinar series. I would also work with the group’s leadership to connect these events to sustained research collaboration. I would like to pilot small, mentored working groups that bring together statisticians, AI researchers, and domain scientists. These groups would develop open case studies on uncertainty quantification, distribution shift, and reliable decision-making. Each would aim to produce a reproducible analysis and practical evaluation guidance. Students and early-career researchers would take meaningful organizing roles. Joint activities with SLDS and other ASA communities would broaden participation. Together, these efforts would help translate statistical ideas into tools for building and evaluating trustworthy AI.
